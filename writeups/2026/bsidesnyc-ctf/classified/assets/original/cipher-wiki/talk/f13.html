Surveillance plot accuracy — Snowden context
Morthwaite — 25 Apr 2024, 08:00
No Such Agency was released in October 2013. The Snowden disclosures began in June 2013. The film's core plot — a signals intelligence contractor with access to allied nations' metadata being used as a weapon — is almost precisely what Snowden described. The script was locked before June 2013. — Morthwaite
pellucid_k — 25 Apr 2024, 08:45
Pre-production began in 2011. The script was in its fourth draft by late 2012. I've seen the Writers Guild registration date in an interview with the screenwriter. The film described the surveillance architecture before it was publicly known to exist. — pellucid_k
WardWatcher_99 — 25 Apr 2024, 09:30
This is the one that makes me uncomfortable every time. I can explain away the tech accuracy in earlier films. I cannot explain this one away. — WardWatcher_99
BrumbyFilm — 25 Apr 2024, 10:00
Good screenwriters research. The surveillance state had been written about extensively before Snowden. The Guardian had been covering GCHQ for years. The film extrapolated from public knowledge. It's not mysterious. — BrumbyFilm
Morthwaite — 25 Apr 2024, 10:45
Of course. — Morthwaite
