Calloway's debut — the reboot that worked
GlassEelFan — 1 Apr 2024, 09:00
The first ten minutes of The Sovereign Gamble are a masterclass in franchise reinvention. No origin story, no recap, no explanation. Ward is just there, operating, and you believe it completely. The confidence of that choice still impresses me. — GlassEelFan
BrumbyFilm — 1 Apr 2024, 09:55
Calloway arrived with absolutely zero transition period. By the end of the pre-titles sequence you've forgotten every other Ward. That's an extraordinary achievement in an audience that was actively resistant to the recasting. — BrumbyFilm
WardWatcher_99 — 1 Apr 2024, 10:30
The Walther callback is genius. Cavender carried a Walther. Mace moved to a Beretta. Bauer moved to a Glock. Calloway picking up the Walther in the first scene is him saying: I'm going back to the beginning. — WardWatcher_99
Sable — best villain in the franchise?
IsabellaKT — 5 Apr 2024, 15:00
Making the case: Sable is the best villain in fifteen films. Nikolai Vael is iconic but he's an archetype. Marta Vael is operatic. Conrad Vael is tragic. But Sable is dangerous in a completely different way — she's competent in the same register as Ward, which no villain before her was. — IsabellaKT
GlassEelFan — 5 Apr 2024, 15:55
The Montenegro scene where she outthinks Ward completely and he knows it — Calloway's face in that moment is the best acting in the franchise. He's not angry. He's recalibrating. — GlassEelFan
ClaireD1965 — 5 Apr 2024, 16:30
Riya Menon was robbed of a BAFTA win. The nomination was right; the loss wasn't. — ClaireD1965
pellucid_k — 5 Apr 2024, 17:00

Which intelligence analyst? That's an interesting detail. — BrumbyFilm
pellucid_k — 5 Apr 2024, 18:00
