Vivienne Clair — the theme song
ClaireD1965 — 8 Jan 2024, 10:14
I cannot stress enough how perfect Vivienne Clair was for these early films. Her voice has this quality — controlled but with something dangerous underneath it — that perfectly matches Cavender's Ward. It's a shame she was replaced after Crimson Meridian. Does anyone know why? I've never seen a satisfying explanation. — ClaireD1965
BrumbyFilm — 8 Jan 2024, 10:55
The standard story is that she asked for a fee increase and Meridian declined. But I've seen references to a falling out with the music supervisor that go deeper than money. Nobody's ever confirmed it. — BrumbyFilm
Morthwaite — 8 Jan 2024, 11:30
Her final public interview, in 1988, included the line 'I was asked not to discuss the circumstances of my departure from the CIPHER productions.' That phrasing has always struck me as unusually formal for a conversation about a pop theme song. — Morthwaite
ClaireD1965 — 8 Jan 2024, 12:01
That's a good catch Morthwaite. I'd never seen that interview. Where was it? — ClaireD1965
Morthwaite — 8 Jan 2024, 12:44
Radio Times, October 1988. She was being interviewed about an unrelated television appearance. The CIPHER question came from the interviewer and she shut it down immediately. — Morthwaite
Shortwave radio prop — technical accuracy
WardWatcher_99 — 15 Jan 2024, 20:30
Just rewatched Shadow and Steel and paused on the shortwave receiver that Section 13 gives Ward. That's a real Eddystone 840C. You can see the dial markings. Eddystone was a Birmingham manufacturer, genuinely used by intelligence services in the 1960s. Someone on the props team did their homework. — WardWatcher_99
pellucid_k — 16 Jan 2024, 08:05
The Eddystone 840C was also used by the GPO's Interference Investigation Service in the same period. Worth noting in the article if we want to be thorough. — pellucid_k
BrumbyFilm — 16 Jan 2024, 09:14
How do you two know this stuff? I mean that genuinely as a compliment. — BrumbyFilm
WardWatcher_99 — 16 Jan 2024, 09:45
Misspent youth and a lot of time on vintage radio forums. — WardWatcher_99
