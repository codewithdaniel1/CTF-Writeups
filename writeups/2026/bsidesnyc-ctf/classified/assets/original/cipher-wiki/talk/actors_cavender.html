Timeline discrepancy — RADA and National Service
Morthwaite — 3 Feb 2024, 08:00
The article states Cavender attended RADA from 1951 to 1953 and completed National Service with the Grenadier Guards. The standard RADA programme is two years. National Service was two years. If he completed both sequentially he would have been occupied from 1951 to 1955 before any professional work. But his first professional stage credit is 1954. The timeline doesn't quite fit. — Morthwaite
BrumbyFilm — 3 Feb 2024, 09:00
He could have done National Service before RADA. The article doesn't specify the order. — BrumbyFilm
Morthwaite — 3 Feb 2024, 09:45
It implies RADA first — the paragraph structure suggests it. Worth clarifying in the article either way. — Morthwaite
IsabellaKT — 3 Feb 2024, 10:30
I've reordered the paragraph to make it ambiguous. It was sloppy writing. The order isn't confirmed in any source I can find. — IsabellaKT
