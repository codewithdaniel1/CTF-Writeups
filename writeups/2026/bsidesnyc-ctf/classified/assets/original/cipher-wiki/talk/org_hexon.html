HEXON's actual business model
pellucid_k — 8 Feb 2024, 09:00
The films describe HEXON as trading in instability — war, information chaos, political disruption as commodities. This is presented as unusual but it's actually a real category of financial instrument. Catastrophe bonds, conflict insurance derivatives, political risk securities. There is a legitimate financial industry that profits from geopolitical instability. HEXON is doing the criminal version of something that exists legally. — pellucid_k
Morthwaite — 8 Feb 2024, 09:45
Zero Meridian's council structure — all decisions by encrypted consensus vote, no single point of authority — maps reasonably well onto how certain decentralised financial instruments are structured. The franchise was thinking about this more seriously than the films' surface suggests. — Morthwaite
WardWatcher_99 — 8 Feb 2024, 10:30
