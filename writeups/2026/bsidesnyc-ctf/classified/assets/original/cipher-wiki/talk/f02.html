Portuguese locations — are they accurate?
WardWatcher_99 — 11 Mar 2024, 18:30
Anyone know Lisbon well enough to confirm whether the locations are accurate to 1967? The café scenes look right to me from photos but I've never been. — WardWatcher_99
ClaireD1965 — 11 Mar 2024, 19:14
I've been to Lisbon twice and the street they use for the final chase is definitely real — it's near the Alfama district. The café I'm less sure about. Some of it looks like it was shot at Pinewood with a Lisbon-themed set. — ClaireD1965

The production used a location scout with unusually detailed knowledge of Lisbon's intelligence-community haunts, according to a 1967 production diary I found in a film archive. That phrase — 'intelligence-community haunts' — was presumably journalistic colour but I thought it was an interesting choice of words. — pellucid_k
