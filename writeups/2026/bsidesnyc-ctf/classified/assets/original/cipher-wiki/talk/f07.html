The Conrad reveal — best moment in the franchise?
GlassEelFan — 20 Jan 2024, 19:00
I'll make the case: Ward pulling out the photograph at the end of Shallow Graves is the single best moment across all fifteen CIPHER films. It's been there from the beginning. The audience doesn't know it's significant. Ward doesn't make a big deal of it. And then it destroys everything. — GlassEelFan
WardWatcher_99 — 20 Jan 2024, 19:45
Hard to argue. It's the payoff to something that was never set up as a setup. Which makes it even better. The photograph was in the background of a scene in Dead Reckoning. Nobody noticed. — WardWatcher_99
BrumbyFilm — 20 Jan 2024, 20:30
Wait, it's in Dead Reckoning? I've never caught that. — BrumbyFilm
WardWatcher_99 — 20 Jan 2024, 21:00
About 34 minutes in, on the desk in Ward's Paris safe house. It's blurry and out of focus. I'm not even sure it was intentional. But it's there. — WardWatcher_99
IsabellaKT — 21 Jan 2024, 08:00
Even if it wasn't intentional it's a beautiful accident. The best kind of retroactive continuity. — IsabellaKT
Chrome Vector theme — period context
ClaireD1965 — 25 Jan 2024, 10:00
The Chrome Vector theme for Shallow Graves is absolutely a product of its moment — that gated reverb drum sound, the synth pads — but it holds up better than most of its contemporaries. And it charted. A CIPHER theme reaching number 4 in the UK is remarkable. The franchise had never had that before. — ClaireD1965
GlassEelFan — 25 Jan 2024, 10:45
The US chart position is interesting too. Number 9 on the Hot 100. CIPHER had never really broken America commercially. That song changed something for the franchise's profile over there. — GlassEelFan
BrumbyFilm — 25 Jan 2024, 11:30
Damian Forde's cameo in the casino scene is delightfully self-aware. He's clearly enjoying himself. — BrumbyFilm
Mace's final scene — where does Ward go?
pellucid_k — 30 Jan 2024, 09:00
The film ends with Ward watching Conrad being taken into custody, then getting into a car and driving. We don't see where he goes. The next Ward film is five years later with a new actor. What's the in-universe explanation for the gap? — pellucid_k
IsabellaKT — 30 Jan 2024, 09:45
The gap is generally taken as Ward being stood down after the Conrad operation. The emotional toll of the reveal — that HEXON had been operating for years while Sentinel was looking the wrong way — is implied to have required a long recovery. The Sovereign Gamble nods to this with Ward's 'reinstatement.' — IsabellaKT
Morthwaite — 30 Jan 2024, 10:30
There is also the question of what happened in the 1987-1991 period in-universe. The franchise skips it entirely. Given that this period includes some of the most significant intelligence events of the twentieth century, that's a notable gap. — Morthwaite
