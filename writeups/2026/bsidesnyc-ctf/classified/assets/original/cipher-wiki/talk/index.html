Halcyon Group — who actually are they?
BrumbyFilm — 14 March 2024, 14:22
Does anyone know anything about the Halcyon Group beyond the production credit? I've been trying to find a company history and there's essentially nothing. No interviews with executives, no other productions, no industry profiles. For a company that's been producing one of Britain's biggest film franchises for sixty years, they are remarkably invisible. — BrumbyFilm
IsabellaKT — 14 March 2024, 15:08
They've always been that way. Edmund Hale reportedly structured it deliberately to keep the creative and financial sides completely separate. Some tax arrangement apparently. Very common in British film in the 60s. — IsabellaKT
BrumbyFilm — 14 March 2024, 15:44
I've seen that explanation before but it doesn't quite hold up. Tax shelter structures from the 60s were dissolved or restructured decades ago. Halcyon Group still files as a private limited company with no public-facing directors. I'm not saying anything sinister, it's just unusual. — BrumbyFilm
VaelArchiveMod — 14 March 2024, 16:01
This comes up every few years. The simple answer is that Meridian Pictures handles all public-facing operations and Halcyon is essentially a holding vehicle. Nothing to see here. — VaelArchiveMod
Morthwaite — 15 March 2024, 07:14
The Halcyon Group was incorporated in 1963, two years before Shadow and Steel. If it was purely a production vehicle you'd expect it to appear after the first film's success, not before. Whoever set it up knew what they were doing before the first frame was shot. — Morthwaite
ClaireD1965 — 15 March 2024, 08:45
