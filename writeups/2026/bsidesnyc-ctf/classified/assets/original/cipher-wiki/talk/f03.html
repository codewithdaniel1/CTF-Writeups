East Berlin authenticity
pellucid_k — 19 Feb 2024, 11:00
The East Berlin sequences in this film have always struck me as unusually authentic. The signage, the architecture, the checkpoint procedures. Most Western films of this era got the aesthetics badly wrong. Agent Alone gets them right. — pellucid_k
Morthwaite — 19 Feb 2024, 11:45
The checkpoint protocol shown in the film matches the documented procedure at Checkpoint Bravo in 1969-1970. Not Checkpoint Charlie — Bravo. That's an unusual level of specificity. Checkpoint Charlie is what Western productions always used. Bravo was less documented. — Morthwaite
WardWatcher_99 — 19 Feb 2024, 12:30
Where are you getting the Checkpoint Bravo documentation from? That sounds like an archive deep dive. — WardWatcher_99
Morthwaite — 19 Feb 2024, 13:15
Stasi records. A portion were digitised and made available through the German Federal Archives in 2018. The overlap with the film's depiction is notable. Probably coincidence. — Morthwaite

Morthwaite I genuinely love that you spend your free time cross-referencing 1970s spy films against declassified Stasi records. CipherWiki is lucky to have you. — IsabellaKT
Morthwaite — 19 Feb 2024, 14:30
It's a hobby. — Morthwaite
