Is Marta actually dead?
WardWatcher_99 — 8 Nov 2023, 21:00
The article states Marta's death as 'apparent.' I want to push back on even that qualifier. We never see a body. The explosion is offscreen. In a franchise that has played with apparent deaths repeatedly, I think we should be stronger in saying the question is deliberately left open. — WardWatcher_99
IsabellaKT — 8 Nov 2023, 21:45
Elise Fontaigne confirmed in a 2001 interview that she was told by Raymond Holt that Marta was dead 'as far as the story was concerned.' That's about as close to confirmation as we're going to get. — IsabellaKT

Cavender's farewell — underrated performance?
ClaireD1965 — 12 Nov 2023, 10:00
I've been thinking about this a lot since rewatching. Cavender's final scene — sitting in the car outside Sentinel, not going in — is one of the great unspoken endings in the franchise. He doesn't say a word. You just watch him decide to go back. It's remarkable. — ClaireD1965
IsabellaKT — 12 Nov 2023, 10:45
Completely agree. The whole third act of Crimson Meridian is Cavender operating at a level the franchise rarely matched again until Calloway. The moment where Ward realises Sentinel was involved — he doesn't explode. He just gets very, very quiet. — IsabellaKT

NATO KL-7 prop — accuracy
Morthwaite — 20 Nov 2023, 08:00
The KL-7 cipher machine prop used in Crimson Meridian is accurate to the KL-7 ADONIS configuration used by NATO in 1973. The rotor count is correct. The key list format shown briefly on screen matches declassified NSA documentation. This is not the kind of detail a props department invents. Someone provided it. — Morthwaite
BrumbyFilm — 20 Nov 2023, 09:00
How on earth do you know what the key list format looks like? — BrumbyFilm
Morthwaite — 20 Nov 2023, 09:45
NSA released a FOIA package in 2015 covering KL-7 documentation. The similarities are there if you look. — Morthwaite
GlassEelFan — 20 Nov 2023, 10:30
Adding this to the technical accuracy section. Good find. — GlassEelFan
