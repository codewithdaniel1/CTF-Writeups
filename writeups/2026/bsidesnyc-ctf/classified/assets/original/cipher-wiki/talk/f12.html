The steganography plot — was it realistic?
Morthwaite — 10 Apr 2024, 08:00
The steganographic activation mechanism in Pale Fire — hiding instructions in LSB data in a publicly posted JPEG — is technically accurate. More importantly, it was accurate in 2008 when the script was written, before this technique was widely discussed in mainstream security coverage. The film described an attack method before most people knew it existed. — Morthwaite
WardWatcher_99 — 10 Apr 2024, 09:00
The specific photo-sharing platform they use in the film is clearly Flickr. Which was the dominant platform in 2008-2009. Using a mainstream consumer platform for covert message distribution is exactly the kind of thing that real-world intelligence services were exploring at that time. — WardWatcher_99
pellucid_k — 10 Apr 2024, 09:45
The decoder software shown has a UI that matches Section 13's general design language across the franchise — dark background, mono-spaced font, minimal chrome. Whoever designs Section 13 equipment for props has consistent taste. — pellucid_k
BrumbyFilm — 10 Apr 2024, 10:30
I just want to point out that we have, on this talk page, three people who understand steganography at a level that slightly worries me. — BrumbyFilm
Iris Vo as Thirteen — debut assessment
IsabellaKT — 15 Apr 2024, 14:00
Iris Vo's introduction as Thirteen is handled perfectly. No big announcement, no fanfare. She's just there, behind the desk, and it's immediately clear she's more formidable than anyone who came before. The scene where she tells Ward the new equipment 'isn't rated for the kind of operational choices you tend to make' is instantly iconic. — IsabellaKT
GlassEelFan — 15 Apr 2024, 14:55
Every Thirteen has had a different relationship with Ward. Fry's Thirteen was paternal. Reyes's was collegial. Vo's is something like adversarial respect. She doesn't like him much but she respects what he does. That tension is excellent. — GlassEelFan
ClaireD1965 — 15 Apr 2024, 15:30
The Thirteen character has consistently been the best-written supporting role in the franchise. Whoever holds that brief in the writers' room is doing exceptional work. — ClaireD1965
