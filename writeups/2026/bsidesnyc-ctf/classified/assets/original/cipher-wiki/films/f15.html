The Last Directorate is film #15 in the CIPHER franchise (2023), directed by Clara Nwosu and starring Marcus Obi as Declan Ward.

Plot

Obi's debut. Liora Vael — Dominic's sister, long believed dead — emerges with evidence that Sentinel itself was complicit in HEXON's founding in 1952. Ward must decide whether to protect the institution that made him or burn it down to save what it was supposed to stand for. The film ends ambiguously. The franchise's best-reviewed entry since The Sovereign Gamble, and its highest-grossing to date.

Theme Song

Theme Song
"Never Belonged"
Performed by Marcus Obi — 2023
In an unprecedented move, lead actor Marcus Obi performed the theme himself — a decision announced without prior publicity. Obi, who trained as a singer before acting, co-wrote the song with composer Adisa Kone. It debuted at #1 in the UK and was described by the BBC as 'the most complete artistic statement in the franchise's history.'

Section 13 Equipment

Gadgets & Technology
  • AI-assisted voice synthesis detector — identifies deepfake audio by analysing micro-artefacts in the waveform invisible to human hearing.
  • Zero-day exploit kit — packaged on a USB drive; bridges air-gapped systems by exploiting firmware vulnerabilities in common peripheral hardware.
  • Encrypted mesh communicator — peer-to-peer, no central server; each device relays and re-encrypts messages, making traffic analysis effectively impossible.
  • Biometric spoof kit — synthetic fingerprint overlays and iris-pattern contact lenses; allows Ward to pass as a registered Sentinel operative under a false identity.
