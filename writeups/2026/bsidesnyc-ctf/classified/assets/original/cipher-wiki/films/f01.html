Shadow and Steel is film #1 in the CIPHER franchise (1965), directed by Gerald Forsythe and starring Hugh Cavender as Declan Ward.

Plot

Ward's first mission: disrupt a Soviet plan to insert a sleeper network into British industry using encrypted shortwave broadcasts hidden inside commercial radio signals. The hunt takes him from London to Istanbul to a steel foundry outside Bratislava. He faces Nikolai Vael for the first time — and lets him escape. The film establishes the franchise's moral ambiguity: Ward wins the battle and loses the man.

Theme Song

Theme Song
"Shadow and Steel"
Performed by Vivienne Clair — 1965
The first of four consecutive CIPHER themes performed by Clair, whose smoky contralto became synonymous with the franchise's early identity. Orchestrated by Edwin Rowe.

Section 13 Equipment

Gadgets & Technology
  • One-Time Pad cipher kit — housed in a bespoke leather wallet; each page chemically treated to dissolve in water within 30 seconds of exposure.
  • Shortwave radio receiver — disguised as a commercially available transistor radio; Section 13 modifications isolate sub-carrier signals hidden within standard broadcast frequencies.
  • Microdot camera — concealed in a cufflink; photographs a full document page reduced to a pinhead-sized image.
Vehicles
  • 1964 Jaguar E-Type Series 1 — a pre-production variant sourced by Section 13, with reinforced sills and a concealed document compartment behind the dashboard.
Weapons
  • Suppressed Walther PPK — Ward's sidearm throughout the Cavender era.
  • Garotte wire — integrated into the lining of Ward's wristwatch strap.
