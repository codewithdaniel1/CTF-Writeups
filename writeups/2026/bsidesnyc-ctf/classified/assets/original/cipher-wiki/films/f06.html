Dead Reckoning is film #6 in the CIPHER franchise (1982), directed by Victor Ashmore and starring Roland Mace as Declan Ward.

Plot

A Sentinel safe house network has been compromised — someone has been feeding HEXON addresses through an early computer terminal network. Ward must trace the breach through a chain of mainframe relay stations across Europe before the last safe house burns. The film is notable for its surprisingly accurate depiction of early networked computing and its cold, procedural tension.

Theme Song

Theme Song
"Running Dark"
Performed by Marco Valence — 1982
A taut, synthesiser-driven theme by Belgian-born composer Marco Valence. Valence had previously scored two European thrillers and brought a cold-war electronic texture to the franchise.

Section 13 Equipment

Gadgets & Technology
  • Portable terminal with acoustic coupler modem — Ward connects to mainframe relay systems by placing a telephone handset into the coupler; Section 13 firmware allows navigation of proprietary command interfaces.
  • Data cassette tape — Section 13 encrypted payload at 300 baud; resembles a standard commercial C-60 audio tape.
  • Signal tracer — follows telephone line routing through analogue exchange boards; displayed on a small LED readout embedded in what appears to be a standard multimeter.
Vehicles
  • 1981 Jaguar XJ6 Series III — reinforced chassis; police-band radio scanner concealed behind the centre console fascia.
Weapons
  • Suppressed Heckler & Koch P7
  • Steel cable restraint system — concealed within the lining of Ward's briefcase; deploys via a hidden lever in the handle.
