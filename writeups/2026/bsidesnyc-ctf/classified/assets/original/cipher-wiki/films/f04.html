Crimson Meridian is film #4 in the CIPHER franchise (1973), directed by Raymond Holt and starring Hugh Cavender as Declan Ward.

Plot

HEXON brokers a deal between three rival intelligence agencies to share a stolen NATO encryption key. Ward must intercept the handoff before the key changes hands — but discovers Sentinel itself may have been involved in the original theft. Marta Vael returns, and the film ends with her apparent death. Cavender's final outing as Ward: considered his finest performance.

Section 13 Equipment

Gadgets & Technology
  • NATO KL-7 cipher machine replica — a fully functional prop built by Section 13 to interface with captured HEXON communications; rotor configuration changed daily.
  • Magnetic strip reader — skims encoded data from access control ID cards at close range for later cloning.
  • Pocket microfilm reader — a Section 13 prototype; standard handheld device of the era reduced to coat-pocket size.
Vehicles
  • 1972 Jaguar XJ12 — armoured B-pillar and doors; hidden document compartment beneath the rear seat; police-band radio concealed in the centre console.
Weapons
  • Suppressed Walther PPK — recurring sidearm.
  • Shoulder holster — Section 13 custom, with a concealed secondary magazine embedded in the harness.
