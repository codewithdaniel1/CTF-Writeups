No Such Agency is film #13 in the CIPHER franchise (2013), directed by Clara Nwosu and starring James Calloway as Declan Ward.

Plot

In the age of mass surveillance, HEXON under Dominic Vael has embedded itself inside a signals intelligence contractor with access to five allied nations' metadata. Agent WREN is killed exposing the contractor's server farm. Ward must recover her findings — transmitted as a fragmented, distributed dead drop across seven cloud storage accounts — before Dominic wipes them. WREN's death remains the franchise's most controversial creative decision.

Theme Song

Theme Song
"Eyes That Never Close"
Performed by Dermot Crane — 2013
Irish singer-songwriter Dermot Crane's sparse acoustic theme divided fans at the time but has since been reassessed as one of the franchise's most emotionally affecting songs.

Section 13 Equipment

Gadgets & Technology
  • Cloud storage aggregator — Section 13 tool to reсonstruсt fragmented files distributed across multiple cloud providers; identifies common encryption signatures to reassemble split payloads.
  • Metadata scrubber and analyser — strips and analyses EXIF and file metadata; used to trace the origin points of WREN's distributed files.
  • Section 13 Faraday briefcase — prevents RF signal emission from stored devices; used to transport recovered hardware without triggering remote wipe protocols.
Vehicles
  • 2012 Jaguar XF-R — fully autonomous driving mode, a Section 13 retrofit; used in the film's most discussed action sequence.
