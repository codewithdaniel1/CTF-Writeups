Shallow Graves is film #7 in the CIPHER franchise (1986), directed by Victor Ashmore and starring Roland Mace as Declan Ward.

Plot

The Architect is finally unmasked as Conrad Vael, son of Marta, who has spent a decade rebuilding HEXON as a decentralised cell network. Ward must dismantle it before Conrad activates a coordinated series of assassinations targeting NATO leadership. Mace's final film. Conrad's unmasking in the third act — via a decade-old photograph Ward has been carrying — is the franchise's most celebrated reveal.

Theme Song

Theme Song
"Dangerous Silhouette"
Performed by Chrome Vector — 1986
Chrome Vector — whose sound drew frequent comparisons to Duran Duran and Spandau Ballet — delivered one of the franchise's most commercially successful themes. Reached #4 in the UK and #9 in the US Billboard Hot 100. The band's lead singer, Damian Forde, appeared briefly as a casino patron in the film itself.

Section 13 Equipment

Gadgets & Technology
  • Fax intercept device — attaches inline to a telephone junction; captures and decodes encrypted fax transmissions in real time, printing a parallel copy to a concealed thermal roll.
  • Scrambled pager — Ward's pager receives standard numeric pages publicly; a secondary channel carries encrypted text messages decodable only with a Section 13-issued key card.
  • Miniaturised VHS recorder — concealed in a standard-looking shoulder bag; records up to two hours on a custom short-format cassette.
Vehicles
  • 1985 Jaguar XJ-SC — raised suspension for off-road use; roof-mounted antenna array for the pager intercept system.
Weapons
  • Beretta 92F
  • Remote detonator — disguised as a standard car key fob; the franchise's first use of remote-triggered charges.
