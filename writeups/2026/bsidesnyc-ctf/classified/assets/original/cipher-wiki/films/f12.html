Pale Fire is film #12 in the CIPHER franchise (2009), directed by Søren Haak and starring James Calloway as Declan Ward.

Plot

Sable is revealed as HEXON's director. Ward and Nadia Reyes pursue her across Eastern Europe as she activates a sleeper network recruited in the 1990s — ordinary people, never previously activated, given a single instruction via a steganographic image posted to a public photo-sharing website. The franchise's first engagement with social media as an intelligence tool. Iris Vo debuts as the new Thirteen.

Theme Song

Theme Song
"Pale Fire (Song)"
Performed by Soraya Nile — 2009
Nile returned for the second Calloway film with a quieter, more intimate theme — a deliberate contrast to the grandeur of The Sovereign Gamble. Produced by Ennio Bracci.

Section 13 Equipment

Gadgets & Technology
  • Steganographic image decoder — Section 13 software; extracts hidden data payloads from JPEG files by analysing least-significant-bit patterns across the image.
  • Social media OSINT toolkit — Sentinel's proprietary aggregator; cross-references accounts across platforms against known HEXON contact patterns.
  • Encrypted VoIP communicator — disguised as a standard Nokia handset of the era; routes calls through a rotating series of Sentinel relay nodes.
Vehicles
  • 2008 Jaguar XF — full Section 13 electronics suite; the navigation system doubles as a signals intelligence receiver.
Weapons
  • Glock 19
  • Section 13 taser — disguised as a contemporary Nokia mobile phone.
