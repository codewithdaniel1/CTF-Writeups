Blacksite is film #10 in the CIPHER franchise (2002), directed by Søren Haak and starring Stefan Bauer as Declan Ward.

Plot

Post-9/11. Sentinel is pressured to share intelligence with allied agencies. Ward discovers a HEXON Council operative embedded inside a joint task force, using shared databases to identify and eliminate informants. The film is the darkest of the Bauer era — surveillance as a weapon turned inward. Bauer's final film ends with Ward resigning from Sentinel, a resignation that is quietly reversed offscreen before The Sovereign Gamble.

  • Biometric data card — forged Sentinel credentials with embedded fingerprint data; allows Ward to pass biometric checkpoints under a false identity.

  • USB dead drop device — disguised as a hotel key card; stores encrypted data payloads for physical handoff without direct contact between agents.
