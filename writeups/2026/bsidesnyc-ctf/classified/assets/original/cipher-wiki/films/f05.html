The Ivory Gambit is film #5 in the CIPHER franchise (1977), directed by Alan Birch and starring Roland Mace as Declan Ward.

Plot

Ward is sent to retrieve a defecting Soviet chess grandmaster who has memorised an entire HEXON communications codebook. The mission spans Vienna, Nairobi, and Hong Kong. Mace's debut brings a lighter touch — more wit, more spectacle — and introduces the unnamed villain known only as The Architect, operating through intermediaries. The codebook, hidden in the grandmaster's annotated chess notation, is the film's central MacGuffin.

Theme Song

Theme Song
"Eyes of the Ivory King"
Performed by The Silverline — 1977
A soul-inflected theme by British group The Silverline, marking the franchise's tonal shift under Roland Mace. Reached #12 in the UK singles chart.

Section 13 Equipment

Gadgets & Technology
  • Chess notation cipher decoder — a purpose-built Section 13 device resembling a standard chess clock; interprets HEXON's codebook methodology from annotated game records.
  • Laser microphone — reads vibrations from window glass at up to 200 metres; assembled from components disguised as camera equipment.
  • Compact satellite uplink terminal — briefcase-sized; in 1977, represented the frontier of portable communications technology. Used for encrypted voice relay via the Sentinel satellite network.
Vehicles
  • 1976 Jaguar XJ-S — pursuit-tuned suspension, hidden compartment behind the rear seat, short-range signal jammer integrated into the dashboard.
Weapons
  • EMP pulse device — integrated into Ward's wristwatch; short range, disables electronic locks and vehicle ignitions.
  • Tranquilliser dart cufflinks — spring-loaded, single shot each.
