After the Wall is film #8 in the CIPHER franchise (1991), directed by Peter Lindqvist and starring Stefan Bauer as Declan Ward.

Plot

The Cold War is over. Ward is sent into a reunified Berlin to recover HEXON's archive before Conrad Vael's former lieutenants can sell it to the highest bidder. Bauer's debut — quieter, more interior than his predecessors. Miriam Alade takes over as Control, in a succession scene that received a standing ovation at the London premiere. The archive turns out to be a single encrypted hard drive.

Theme Song

Theme Song
"After the Fall"
Performed by Lena Voß — 1991
Austrian vocalist Lena Voß was selected partly as a nod to Stefan Bauer's nationality. Her theme is an austere, post-wall meditation with minimal instrumentation.

Section 13 Equipment

Gadgets & Technology
  • Encrypted hard drive — 40MB capacity, encrypted with a proprietary Section 13 algorithm; recovering its contents forms the film's central technical challenge.
  • Section 13-modified Toshiba T3200 laptop — running custom decryption software; the prop is an actual production unit sourced and modified for filming.
  • Miniature DAT recorder — digital audio tape; far superior fidelity to the analogue recorders of previous films; small enough to be concealed in an inside jacket pocket.
Vehicles
  • 1990 Jaguar XJ40 — deliberately unassuming specification; Section 13 armoured the B-pillar and doors without altering the exterior appearance.
Weapons
  • Glock 17 — Ward's first use of a Glock; noted by fans as a generational shift in the franchise's aesthetic.
