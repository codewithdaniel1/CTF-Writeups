Sentinel Intelligence Directorate (fictional organisation)

The Sentinel Intelligence Directorate — referred to in-universe simply as Sentinel, or by field agents as "the Directorate" — is the fictional British intelligence body that employs Declan Ward. It operates outside the formal oversight structures of MI5 and MI6, established by royal warrant in 1952 under circumstances that remain sealed until 2047.

Overview

Sentinel does not appear in any public record. Its budget is folded into three separate ministries. The Director answers to the Prime Minister and, theoretically, the Crown — though the precise nature of that relationship has never been tested in writing. Field agents refer to the organisation simply as "the Directorate." Saying the full name in the field is considered poor tradecraft — and, among older agents, bad luck.

Section 13

Section 13 is Sentinel's technical and equipment division, responsible for the DESign, testing, and field deployment of all specialist tools, weapons, vehicles, and communications technology used by Sentinel operatives. It has no official name beyond the number — early organisational charts list it as "Section 13 — Applied Research," an addition made by a Deputy Director in 1963 that field agents have ignored ever since.

Section 13 is housed in a converted Victorian waterworks beneath Vauxhall, acquired by Sentinel in 1954 and absent from any property register. Staff refer to the building as "the Pipe."

The head of Section 13 carries the informal field title Thirteen, used by Ward and other senior operatives. No Section 13 chief has ever used the title themselves, considering it undignified.

Complicity controversy

In The Last Directorate (2023), Liora Vael presents evidence that Sentinel was complicit in HEXON's founding in 1952. Whether this constitutes a retcon of franchise canon or a revelation consistent with earlier, ambiguous hints is the subject of ongoing fan discussion. The film does not resolve the question definitively.
